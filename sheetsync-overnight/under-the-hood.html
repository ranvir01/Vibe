SheetSync Overnight

Overnight inbox → current Master, with human gates

Synthetic data Internal Facing × LLM-Based Automated Workflow Human gates G1 · G2 · G3

The idea in four lines

  1. Mail hits the company inbox overnight.
  2. SheetSync Overnight reads it and keeps the spreadsheet Master current: new rows, patched cells.
  3. It stays quiet if nothing changed.
  4. Money, outside sends, logins and rule changes stay human.

Human gates: steps the system does not perform

G1 consequential sends & money (email a counterparty, commit a price, invoice, pay)

G2 human-presence checks (fresh login, 2FA, captcha, payment rails; the run stops and waits)

G3 change the workflow's rules or scope

Policy

Reversible and in scope → automated; irreversible, outward-facing or scope-changing → gated.

Every message, every night, ends in one of three states: patched (incl. created) · quiet · waiting on human.
